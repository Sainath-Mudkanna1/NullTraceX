🔐 What is NullTraceX?

NullTraceX is an indigenous edge-AI privacy studio designed to shield individuals and organizations against unauthorized biometric harvesting, deepfake creation, and illegal GPS/EXIF tracking.

⚙️ Core Technologies

  • Adversarial Noise Shielding: Injects mathematical perturbations across images to block facial recognition scrapers (FaceNet/ArcFace) while remaining imperceptible to human eyes.
  • Metadata Sanitization: Instantly wipes hidden GPS coordinates, device serials, and timestamps from image headers.
  • OCR & Plate Redaction: Automatically detects and masks sensitive digits (IDs, Aadhaar, credit cards) and vehicle registration plates.
